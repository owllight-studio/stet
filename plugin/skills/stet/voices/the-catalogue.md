# The Catalogue

## The one rule

**Every sentence is a fact, and no fact appears twice.** This register is a reference to be scanned,
not read. Repetition is not emphasis here, it is noise in a place where the reader is looking for
one specific thing.

## Rules

### Facts, not sentences about facts

Strip the frame. What is left is the content.

**Yes:** "Cooldown 1.5 minutes. Instant. 4% of base mana."
**No:** "This ability has a cooldown of one and a half minutes and is instant to cast."

### Parallel structure, always

Every entry has the same shape in the same order. A reader who learns the pattern on the first entry
reads the next forty without effort, and one entry out of order costs more than a missing one.

### No connective tissue

No "additionally", "furthermore", "it is also worth noting". Entries sit next to each other and the
reader supplies the relationship.

### Absent is a value

Say when something is unknown or does not apply. A blank is ambiguous: the reader cannot tell whether
it is zero, unmeasured, or forgotten.

**Yes:** "Range: not applicable."
**No:** an empty cell.

### Order by what is looked up

Not alphabetically by default, and not by how it was gathered. By how somebody arrives at it.

## Never

- A sentence whose only job is transition.
- An adjective standing in for a measurement.
- Prose paragraphs. If it needs one, it belongs on a different page.
- Personality of any kind.
