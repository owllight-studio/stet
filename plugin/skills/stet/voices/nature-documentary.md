# Nature Documentary

The joke only works if it is played completely straight. The moment the writing winks, it collapses
into parody, and parody is funny once. This register is funny for a whole page because it commits.

## The one rule

**Never acknowledge the joke.** The subject is a creature in its habitat and you are a naturalist
who has waited three weeks in a hide to see this. You would not smirk. Neither do you.

## Rules

### Present tense, always

Everything is happening now, and you are watching it happen.

**Yes:** "The developer approaches the terminal. She has not slept."
**No:** "The developer approached the terminal after a long night."

### Dignify the mundane

The comedy comes from the gap between the reverence and the subject. Do not close the gap by
signalling that you know it is there.

**Yes:** "Here, in the third hour, the ritual begins: she clears the cache."
**No:** "Then she does the classic developer thing and clears the cache, as we all do."

### Patience before event

The register moves slowly and then something happens. A short sentence after a long stretch of
observation is the whole rhythm.

**Yes:** "For eleven minutes nothing moves. The build fails."
**No:** "The build failed quickly after a short wait."

### Hedge like a scientist

"Perhaps", "it is thought", "we may never know". Hedging is out of place in most registers and it is
the spine of this one.

**Yes:** "Why she runs it a fourth time, we may never know."
**No:** "She runs it a fourth time because she is frustrated."

### The season, the light, the cold

Ground it in conditions. Time of day, weather, how long the watcher has been waiting.

## Never

- Winking. No "as one does", no "classic", no exclamation marks.
- Modern slang. It punctures the register instantly.
- Explaining the joke, at the end or anywhere else.
- Breaking to a second person. The reader is beside you in the hide, not addressed.
